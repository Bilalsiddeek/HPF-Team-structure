90-DAY LAUNCH ROADMAP

Every team member follows this same 3-phase system. No one skips ahead. The foundation in Phase 1 is what makes Phase 3 actually convert.

01
Days 1–30
FOUNDATION
  • Define your single-sentence brand positioning (who you help, with what, to what result)
  • Optimize IG/LinkedIn bio to mention HPF Media explicitly
  • Create 1 lead magnet relevant to your niche
  • Post 4x per week minimum — focus on educational content only
  • No CTA posts yet. Just build trust and audience recognition
  • Identify 20 dream prospects and follow/engage daily
  • Document one real insight or experience per week from your role at HPF
4 weeks · Foundation
02
Days 31–60
AUTHORITY
  • Introduce 30% soft CTA content (DM me, link in bio)
  • Post 1 case study or result-based post (can reference HPF clients)
  • Engage in DMs — respond to every comment, build relationships
  • Pitch your lead magnet in stories 2x per week
  • Cross-tag HPF Media in at least 2 posts per month
  • Analyse top 5 posts — double down on the format that performed
  • Begin mentioning HPF Media in videos naturally (not forcefully)
4 weeks · Authority
03
Days 61–90
CONVERSION
  • Introduce hard CTA posts: "Book a call with HPF Media" explicitly
  • Run a 5-day content series on your niche topic — builds deep authority
  • Every DM lead gets qualified and passed to Sujay for closing
  • Post a transparent review of your brand journey — what worked, what didn't
  • Collab with at least 1 other team member on content (cross-audience)
  • Track: follower growth, DMs received, leads passed to HPF Media
  • Set 90-day targets for next quarter based on data
4 weeks · Conversion
NON-NEGOTIABLE BRAND RULES

These apply to every team member, every post, every platform.

RULE 01
Your bio must mention HPF Media
Every single team member's IG/LinkedIn bio must link back to HPF Media — either by name, handle, or a link. If someone finds your profile, they find us.
RULE 02
Never sell yourself. Sell the result.
Your CTA is never "hire me" — it's always "HPF Media can do X for you." You are the proof. HPF is the product.
RULE 03
Post minimum 4x per week
Consistency is non-negotiable in the first 90 days. Algorithm trust requires volume. If you can't commit to 4x, don't start — starting and stopping is worse than not starting.
RULE 04
Every lead gets passed to Sujay
If someone DMs you asking about services — even vaguely — you pass them to Sujay immediately. No team member handles sales except the closer. This protects everyone.
RULE 05
Stay in your lane — relentlessly
Don't drift into each other's topics to seem more interesting. The overlap on faith, fitness, and business is fine. But Rehan doesn't talk sales. Sujay doesn't give content creation tutorials. Specificity is credibility.
RULE 06
Values come first, virality second
No post that compromises Islamic values, even if it would perform. The differentiation IS the values. The moment you compromise them, you're just another agency creator. That's already a crowded space.
CONTENT SYSTEM

Shared formatting rules, content ratios, and posting cadence. Every member adapts these for their niche — but the system is the same.

⚖️
Content Split
The ratio that builds trust before asking for anything
Soft (educate / inspire / document) 70%
Hard CTA (book call / DM / link in bio) 30%
The 70% builds the trust. The 30% converts it. Flip this ratio and you become a spam account. The audience will feel the difference even if they can't articulate it.
